Instagram Influencer Marketing Impact Study

What I Did

  • Conducted survey of 110 participants (91.8% aged 18–25) to assess influencer marketing impact on purchase intention.
  • Applied chi-square, ANOVA, and regression analysis to identify key drivers of consumer behavior.
  • Delivered a 42-page report summarizing statistical findings and actionable insights.

Results

  • Found 70% preferred influencer marketing over traditional methods.
  • Discovered a significant positive correlation between influencer campaigns and purchase intention.

Skills Used

Data Analysis • Chi-Square • ANOVA • Regression • Research • Report Writing • Presentation

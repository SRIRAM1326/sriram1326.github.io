NIFTY 50 Quantitative Analysis Pipeline

What I Did

  • Collected and cleaned 10 years of daily OHLCV data for the NIFTY 50 index.
  • Engineered technical features such as volatility (ATR), volume ratios, and candle structure metrics.
  • Labeled market conditions into Trending and Ranging regimes.
  • Trained a Random Forest machine learning model to identify high-probability trending days.
  • Designed and backtested an ATR-based trading strategy using the model’s predictions.
  • Implemented strict risk management with predefined stop-loss, take-profit, and time-based exits.

Skills Used

Python • Pandas • NumPy • Matplotlib • Seaborn • Scikit-Learn • Finance-Lib

Results

  • Backtested over 1,126 trades across 10 years of data.
  • Achieved positive total P&L (~₹11,000+).
  • Maintained a win rate of ~40%, demonstrating positive expectancy through risk-reward management.
  • Successfully avoided choppy market conditions using machine learning as a market filter.
  • Proved that disciplined risk management can outperform high-accuracy prediction models.
